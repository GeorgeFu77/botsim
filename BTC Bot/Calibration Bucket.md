# Calibration Bucket #btcbot

Buy the favorite whenever its price falls in a fixed bucket (0.5–0.97, six slices) with no other filter — not to make money, but to measure the realized hit-rate at each price and map where the market is miscalibrated.

**Profit: −$237.58** across 6 variants. Best bucket: 0.58–0.66 → **+$87.67**, 63.2% win rate; the high buckets bled.

Its findings feed [[Favorite Sweet Spot]]'s 0.55–0.82 band. [[Longshot Probe]] is the same idea pointed at the cheap side. Part of [[BTC Bot Strategies]].
